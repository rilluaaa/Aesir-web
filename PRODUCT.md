# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is investors evaluating AESIR's research capability, execution record, and long-term relevance. Government, academic, NGO, hospital, and enterprise partners are important secondary audiences.

## Product Purpose

The website presents AESIR as an applied research and development company that bridges human neurodiversity and frontier technology through evidence-based immersive intelligence. Success means a visitor can quickly understand AESIR's research thesis, inspect its three areas of interest, see real-world programs and project media, understand who leads the work, and reach the company through its existing contact page.

## Positioning

AESIR combines data science, human-computer interaction, XR, AI, public policy, and field deployment. Its position is "Immersive Pragmatism": peer-reviewed scientific discovery translated into market-validated, high-impact societal deployment.

## Operating Context

The public site is a React/Vite website deployed on GitHub Pages under the `/Aesir-web/` base path. It contains a large local portfolio archive whose project media and project viewer must remain accessible. The former separate `Led By` / founders route is to be merged into the main AESIR experience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is English-only.
- The three research areas use a selectable research-panel interaction: Society 5.0, AX (AI Transformation), and NEURO Business Futures.
- All existing project media remains accessible.
- The contact destination is `https://aesir.hk/#contactus`.
- There is no investor-specific primary CTA.
- The Three.js meteor and particle background is removed completely.
- Interactive lift, glow, or state change is reserved for controls and media that can actually be opened.
- The page must work across desktop, tablet, and mobile and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Brand Commitments

- The public brand name is AESIR.
- The supplied AESIR logo remains on its white rectangular background; it must not be recolored or made transparent.
- The visual identity is white-led, research-editorial, image-forward, and restrained. Expanded research panels remain light rather than becoming a dark visual interruption.
- Ernest HS CHAN and Zero Yun Wa WONG are presented as Co-Founders.

## Product Principles

- Lead with research credibility, then prove it with field work.
- Separate confirmed evidence from future research intent.
- Make complex research understandable without reducing it to generic technology marketing.
- Keep every legacy project accessible while presenting a curated first view.
- Use real people, real programs, and real artifacts as the visual evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must provide meaningful image alternative text, keyboard-operable research tabs and project controls, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, responsive text and media, and a reduced-motion path.
